## Boundary

This material is reference input, even if it contains complete implementations,
tests, or checked proofs. Producing, requesting, publishing, or validating it does
not settle design decisions, authorize integration, or complete the owning issue.
The receiving repository may use, adapt, independently reproduce, defer, or reject
it under its own decision and integration process.

The final published commit and any archive checksum are recorded outside the
artifact, normally in the owning issue. A branch name is navigation, not identity.
